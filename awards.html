Awards and Recognition

M2IP 2027 recognises outstanding research contributions and effective communication of ideas through two award categories. The top three awardees in each category will be recognised.

🎤

Best Presentation Award

Presented in recognition of participants demonstrating excellence in technical presentation, effective communication, and clarity in conveying research ideas.

Selection Criteria

  • Technical content and understanding of the research problem.
  • Mathematical and conceptual clarity.
  • Presentation skills and effective communication.
  • Quality and organisation of presentation slides.
  • Interpretation and discussion of results.
  • Time management and adherence to the allotted duration.
  • Ability to answer questions during the discussion.

Prizes

🥇 First Prize 🥈 Second Prize 🥉 Third Prize
🏆

Best Research Project Award

Presented to the team demonstrating mathematical clarity, sound implementation, rigorous evaluation, and reproducible results.

Selection Criteria

  • Assessment of the final project report.
  • Evaluation of the submitted implementation code.
  • Mathematical clarity and sound implementation.
  • Rigorous evaluation and reproducibility of results.

Final project report and code must be submitted within 30 days after the summer school.

Note: Well-supported negative findings will receive equal consideration.
