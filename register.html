M2IP 2027

Registration

Summer School on Mathematical Methods in Image Processing
10–20 May 2027 | VIT Chennai

Who Can Apply?

  • Postgraduate students, research scholars, postdoctoral researchers and early-career faculty in Mathematics or related disciplines are eligible to apply.
  • Applicants should have a basic understanding of linear algebra, calculus and probability, and be able to write simple programmes using Python or MATLAB.
  • Prior research experience in image processing or image segmentation is not required.
  • Selected participants must commit to attending the full summer school, actively participating in team-based project activities and completing the 30-day post-summer-school project follow-up.

How Will Candidates Be Selected?

  • Selection will be based on academic merit and a 150–200 word statement of interest, outlining the applicant's academic background, research interests, motivation for attending and expected benefits.
  • Preference will be given to postgraduate students (30%), research scholars (40%) and early-career researchers below 40 years of age (30%).
  • Among equally suitable applicants, preference will be given to candidates with limited access to similar training, considering institutional and geographical diversity.
  • A total of 50 participants will be selected, with a reserve list maintained to fill any vacant seats.

How to Apply?

Interested candidates are invited to submit their applications through the official online application form.

  • Their academic background.
  • Research interests.
  • Motivation for attending the summer school.
  • Expected benefits from participating in M2IP 2027.
