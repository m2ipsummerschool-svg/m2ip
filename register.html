M2IP 2027

Registration

Summer School on Mathematical Methods in Image Processing 10–20 May 2027 | VIT Chennai

Who Can Apply?

  • Postgraduate students, research scholars, postdoctoral researchers and early-career faculty in mathematics or related disciplines.
  • Applicants should have basic knowledge of linear algebra, calculus, and probability, and be able to write simple Python/MATLAB programs.
  • Prior research experience in image processing is not required.
  • Participants must commit to the full school, team assignments, and the 30-day project follow-up.

How Will Candidates Be Selected?

  • Selection will be based on academic merit and a statement of interest of 150–200 words, outlining your academic background, research interests, reasons for attending, and expected benefits.
  • Preference will be given to postgraduate students, research scholars, and early-career researchers below 35 years of age.
  • Among equally suitable applicants, preference will be given to those with limited access to similar training, considering institutional and geographical diversity.
  • 40 participants will be selected, with a reserve list maintained to fill vacant seats.

Fee Refund & Travel Support

  • Participants with at least 90% attendance who complete all prescribed assessments will be eligible for a fee refund, subject to funding availability.
  • Travel support will be subject to funding availability, approved limits, and submission of valid travel documents.
  • Refund conditions and travel support limits will be communicated to selected candidates before fee payment.

Accommodation

Accommodation availability and applicable charges will be communicated in the selection notice.

Accommodation may be provided free of charge, subject to funding availability.

How to Apply?

Interested candidates are invited to submit their applications through the official online application form.

Applicants must provide a statement of interest (150–200 words) describing their academic background, research interests, motivation for attending, and expected benefits.
