📅 May 10–20, 2027 📍 VIT Chennai

The Department of Mathematics, School of Advanced Sciences, VIT Chennai, invites organisations, industry partners and academic institutions to support the Summer School on Mathematical Methods in Image Processing (M2IP 2027) – Cohort 1: Image Segmentation.

M2IP 2027 combines mathematical lectures, hands-on learning and mentored project investigations. Participants will progress from understanding segmentation methods to implementing, evaluating and critically examining them through focused research questions.
