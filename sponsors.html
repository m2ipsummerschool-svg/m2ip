M2IP 2027 | PARTNERSHIP & SUPPORT

Support Research Training at M2IP 2027

Help early-career researchers turn mathematical ideas into meaningful investigations.

Summer School on Mathematical Methods in Image Processing Cohort 1: Image Segmentation May 10–20, 2027 | VIT Chennai

The Department of Mathematics, School of Advanced Sciences, VIT Chennai, invites organisations, industry partners and academic institutions to support M2IP 2027.

The summer school combines expert lectures, hands-on training and mentored projects. Participants will explore the mathematics behind image segmentation, implement methods and investigate their performance, assumptions and limitations.

BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

Support Learning That Continues Beyond the School

Participants will develop focused investigations with a clear problem statement, documented implementation, comparative results and an assessment of limitations. A 30-day post-school refinement period will encourage teams to strengthen their reports and identify directions for further research.

The programme values sound evidence—including carefully verified negative results—and aims to build the habits required for independent research.
