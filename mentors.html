Research Mentorship

The Summer School provides an opportunity for participants to receive research guidance from experienced faculty members. The mentors will help participants understand the research process, identify and develop research problems, explore appropriate methodologies, and organise their findings into a structured scholarly contribution.

Through this mentorship, participants will be guided in developing their research work and preparing it in the form of a book chapter, with an emphasis on research writing, presentation, and academic standards.

From Research Ideas to Book Chapters

The mentorship initiative aims to encourage participants to move beyond classroom learning and engage in structured research activities. With guidance from the mentors, students can work towards developing research ideas, understanding research methodologies, organising their findings, and preparing a well-structured book chapter.
