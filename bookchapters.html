Call for Book Chapters

Turn Your Research Question
into a Book Chapter

A new method. A meaningful comparison. A carefully verified negative result.

Your research journey continues beyond M2IP 2027. Develop your summer school project into a chapter that addresses one distinct image segmentation problem or research question.

EXPLORE YOUR RESEARCH

What Can Your Chapter Explore?

Research is not limited to proposing a new algorithm. A focused question, rigorous investigation and reproducible evidence can make a meaningful contribution.

01

Develop a Method

Propose a mathematically grounded approach or a meaningful improvement to an existing segmentation technique.

02

Compare and Explain

Investigate when segmentation methods succeed, fail or behave differently, and explain the reasons behind their performance.

03

Report a Negative Result

Demonstrate why an approach does not work as expected, supported by carefully documented and reproducible evidence.

04

Test Robustness

Examine the effects of noise, parameter choices, annotation uncertainty or boundary preservation.

05

Solve an Application Problem

Investigate a focused segmentation challenge in biomedical, scientific or industrial imaging.

06

Revisit Published Findings

Reproduce existing research findings and analyse discrepancies, limitations or overlooked observations.
