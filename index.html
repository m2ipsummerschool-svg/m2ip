About the Summer School

The Summer School on Mathematical Methods in Image Processing (M2IP 2027) is designed to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of image segmentation, covering its mathematical foundations, classical approaches, and recent developments in artificial intelligence and deep learning. The programme explores a wide range of segmentation techniques, including clustering-based and fuzzy-based methods, transformer-based architectures, and deep learning approaches.

Over ten days, participants will progressively explore the principles and practical applications of image segmentation, with dedicated sessions on attention-based segmentation models and the Segment Anything Model (SAM). The programme also introduces object detection, YOLO and its variants, providing insights into contemporary approaches for visual recognition and analysis. Through expert lectures, demonstrations, and practical learning sessions, the summer school aims to equip participants with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills to understand and apply modern image segmentation and computer vision techniques.
