4th Summer School on Computer Vision

Date: 01 to 06 July, 2019 | Location: IIIT Hyderabad

For more updates on Summer School include your details here:

JOIN OUR MAILING LIST

Computer Vision is a rapidly evolving field with its applications being steadily integrated into our day to day lives. The field has received a wide interest from various stakeholders ranging from theoretical researchers, application designers and developers, and even business entities. In continuation of our previous summer schools, this year's theme is "Computer Vision".

Scope

The summer school curriculum roughly consists of a series of lectures and demo/lab sessions designed to work in tandem to help you make the most out of the program. Expert talks will be delivered where speakers share their work with attendees, and interaction is strongly encouraged.

Audience

Intended for graduate students working in Computer Vision and related areas. Undergraduate students and industry professionals looking for exposure will also find it beneficial. Basic understanding of Computer Vision is expected.
