About the Summer School

Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence are rapidly evolving fields with applications across science, engineering, healthcare and industry. This summer school brings together students, researchers and practitioners to explore foundational concepts and recent advances.

The programme combines invited lectures, demonstrations and practical sessions, creating opportunities to learn from experts and exchange ideas with fellow participants.

WHO SHOULD ATTEND

Audience

The summer school is intended for postgraduate students, research scholars, faculty members and professionals working in computer vision, machine learning and related areas. Participants should have a basic understanding of the subject; a mathematical and programming background will be helpful.
