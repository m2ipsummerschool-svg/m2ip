WELCOME

About the Summer School

The Summer School on Mathematical Methods in Image Processing (M2IP 2027) is designed to provide participants with a comprehensive understanding of image segmentation, covering its mathematical foundations, classical approaches, and recent developments in artificial intelligence and deep learning. The programme explores a wide range of segmentation techniques, including clustering-based and fuzzy-based methods, transformer-based architectures, and deep learning approaches.

Over ten days, participants will progressively explore the principles and practical applications of image segmentation, with dedicated sessions on attention-based segmentation models and the Segment Anything Model (SAM). The programme also introduces object detection, YOLO and its variants, providing insights into contemporary approaches for visual recognition and analysis. Through expert lectures, demonstrations, and practical learning sessions, the summer school aims to equip participants with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills to understand and apply modern image segmentation and computer vision techniques.

About the School of Advanced Sciences (SAS)

The School of Advanced Sciences (SAS) at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) Chennai comprises the Departments of Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. It offers the following programmes: Ph.D., M.Sc. Data Science, M.Sc. Physics and M.Sc. Chemistry. The school also offers a wide range of core and elective courses facilitating B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, Integrated M.Tech, Management and Law programmes.

OUR INSTITUTION

About Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) was founded in 1984 as Vellore Engineering College, a self-financing institution dedicated to providing quality higher education. In 2001, the Union Ministry of Human Resource Development conferred university status on the institution under Section 3 of the University Grants Commission (UGC) Act, 1956. VIT is led by its visionary founder and Chancellor, Dr. G. Viswanathan, a former Parliamentarian and Minister in the Government of Tamil Nadu. In recognition of his exceptional contributions to education, Dr. G. Viswanathan was awarded an honorary doctorate by West Virginia University (USA) in 2009 and the State University of New York (USA) in 2024. With a commitment to delivering world-class education on par with international standards, VIT continually adopts innovative approaches to teaching and research. Its Memoranda of Understanding with various international universities enable meaningful collaborations through student and faculty exchanges and foster joint research projects. Many of its students, working in international organisations and universities, deliver high-quality solutions and conduct research that bring esteem to India. At VIT, the pursuit of excellence in education and research is complemented by a dynamic ecosystem of innovation, research and global engagement.
